2. Organizational Intelligence, Manufacturing & Supply Chain

Models relationships between manufacturers and commercial partners, builds relationship graphs, and recommends the direction, quantity, and quality of collaboration and incentives.

13

Procurement Augmentation

Analyzes multiple suppliers and market conditions and recommends purchase or collaboration shares in a way designed to maximize the benefit of the participating organizations.

Intelligent Procurement Item Profiles

Analyzes purchases, requisitions, orders, consumption, and inventory; creates a profile for every item and recommends reorder points, upper/lower limits, and ordering timing.

15

Combines historical issues with future possibilities, manages short-term memory, and transfers important items into long-term memory based on importance and interaction.

Value-Creation Logic: These products create the greatest value when organizational data + process + AI model + measurable action are connected within one continuous operating loop.

Page 5 · Recruitment Business Case

AI Recruitment Engine — Business Value

Reduced Screening Time

Accelerates resume screening and the initial interview stage by automating repetitive analysis.

Standardized Evaluation

Applies consistent evaluation criteria across candidates and reduces dependence on momentary interviewer impressions.

Analytics-Ready Recruitment Data

Creates structured evidence that can be analyzed to improve future recruitment decisions and processes.

Integrated Talent Pipeline

Can connect to resumes, assessments, voice/text interviews, and a talent pool or candidate database.

Recommended Operating Principle

The system should produce evidence and recommendations while preserving human accountability for hiring decisions.

Human-in-the-Loop by DesignAI performs structured analysis; managers retain authority over final decisions, exceptions, references, and sensitive judgments.

Page 8 · Investment Summary

7. Investment Case & Recommended Priorities

The value of this portfolio is not determined by the number of tools. Its core differentiation is the transformation of AI from a response generator into an operational and decision-support layer capable of working with proprietary organizational data, retaining context, establishing relationships among data, and connecting outputs to action.

Platform Thesis: Build a modular platform whose common core is a Context + Knowledge + Agent + Action Engine, on top of which specialized products can be deployed — from recruitment and HR to CRM, procurement, knowledge production, contact centers, and personal automation.
